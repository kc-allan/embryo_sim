# Vision

## Purpose

Embryo Sim is an experimental framework for studying how multicellular development and morphogenesis can emerge from explicit local biological and physical mechanisms.

## Audience

Researchers and developers who need interpretable, reproducible computational experiments in developmental systems.

## Winning

Experiments are numerically trustworthy, their assumptions are inspectable, and results can reveal organization, instability, or failure without prescribing anatomy.

## Scope Boundaries

The framework is not a validated human embryo, organogenesis predictor, clinical tool, or guarantee of embryo-like structures.
